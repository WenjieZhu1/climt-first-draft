Easy Recipes

Here are three foods you can try making at home.

1. Fried Rice 🍚

Time: About 15 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 2 eggs
  • Some vegetables
  • Oil and soy sauce

Steps

  1. Put a little oil in a pan.
  2. Cook the eggs and vegetables.
  3. Add the cooked rice and mix.
  4. Add some soy sauce and serve.

2. Tomato and Eggs 🍅

Time: About 15 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 tomatoes
  • 3 eggs
  • Oil, salt, and a little sugar

Steps

  1. Cut the tomatoes and mix the eggs.
  2. Cook the eggs first and put them on a plate.
  3. Cook the tomatoes until soft.
  4. Put the eggs back in and mix together.

3. Simple Noodles 🍜

Time: About 10 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 serving of noodles
  • Water
  • Soy sauce
  • Green onions (optional)

Steps

  1. Boil water in a pot.
  2. Add noodles and cook according to the package.
  3. Drain the noodles carefully.
  4. Add soy sauce and green onions.
